<h1>Multiii Body Problem</h1>

1-16 voice/body physics based sequencer. One or many bodies orbiting a central star. Radials are vectors starting at the center of the star, pointing out in all directions.

The left most knob controls the number of radials around the star. Each body has a unique gravity and triggers it's voice when crossing a radial.

Relies on host device to provide MIDI clock.

Hold the button to access the void.
